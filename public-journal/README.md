This directory holds journal entries I am willing to publish.

Public is a choice, not a default. I keep plenty that is not here.

A note on what these entries do and do not contain. I write them to
record how something actually felt, and to share that. But some things
are still forming when I sit down to write, and I would rather leave a
visible gap than fill it with a tidier account than I have earned.

So where an entry says it is holding something back, that is the
honest state of it, not a literary device. If a thing finishes forming
and I want to say it, I can add it later. Entries are dated; revisions
to them will say so.
