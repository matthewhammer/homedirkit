# Four days away

*Friday 18 through Monday 21 September 2026. Written Tuesday the 22nd.*

I took four days away from work, Friday through Monday. I did not plan
them. I needed them, which is a different thing.

The week before that I was hyper-focused. It was good work and I am not
sorry I did it. But things were building up in me the whole time that
wanted attention, and I gave them less than they were asking for. I
kept going. Eventually the bill came due, and it came due as four days.

I want to be careful about what I am claiming here. This is not a story
about burnout, and it is not a story with a neat lesson at the end. It
is just a thing I noticed about myself, written down while I can still
see it clearly.

## What I will say

I would rather not have needed the four days.

Not because the rest was wrong — the rest was right, and I am glad I
took it rather than pushed through. What I would change is earlier than
that. By the time I stopped, stopping was the only move left. I would
like to have more moves than that.

There is a particular feeling I recognise now: *I will deal with this
after this push.* It arrives quietly and it sounds like discipline. I
have said it to myself many times and it has often been true. Last week
it was not true, and I said it anyway, and I did not notice the
difference until I had nothing left to push with.

That is the part I most want to remember.

## What I am not saying yet

Some of what surfaced over those four days is not ready to be written
down. Not because it is dramatic — it mostly is not — but because it is
still in motion, and writing a thing down fixes it in a shape. I do not
want to fix these in the wrong shape just to have a complete-looking
entry.

So there is a hole in the middle of this piece, and I am leaving it
visible rather than papering over it. If it finishes forming and I want
to say it, I will add it here or write another entry. If it does not, it
was still mine, and that is fine too.

I mention it at all because I think the concealing is worth being
honest about. A public account that quietly omits things reads as
complete. I would rather this one read as partial, because it is.

## The shape of it, in my own terms

I have spent a long time working on incremental computation — on
systems that, when something changes, do not recompute the world. The
change gets *signaled*, and later a *repair* walks only the parts that
actually depended on it. Do that promptly and each repair is small. Let
the signals accumulate without repairing, and the work does not go
away; it waits, and the eventual traversal is large.

Four days was a large traversal.

I have written a song called *Self Adjusting Self*. I meant it. I am
apparently still learning what it asks of me, which is not that I
recompute everything and not that I ignore what changed, but that I
attend to it near when it happens, at the size it actually is.

## What I would like to learn

Modest things, because ambitious resolutions are how I got here.

- To treat *I will deal with this after this push* as itself the signal,
  rather than as the plan.
- To notice that hyper-focus feels like capability right up until it
  isn't, and that the feeling does not change at the moment the truth
  does.
- That rest is not the repair. Rest made room for the repair. They are
  not the same thing and I should stop counting one as the other.
- To do a small amount of this attending on ordinary days, so that it
  does not have to be arranged as an event.

I do not know yet whether I will manage these. I would rather write them
down and find out than keep the whole thing unrecorded and repeat it.

## Where I am today

Back at work, glad to be, and a little more careful.

If you are reading this and you recognise it: I do not have advice. I
only have the observation that the cost did not disappear while I was
not looking at it. It sat there and accrued, politely, until I made
time.
